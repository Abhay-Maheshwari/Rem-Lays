built by someone who kept losing things too.

rem-lays didn't start as a product. It started as a folder of screenshots, a hundred open browser tabs, and a note titled "watch later" that never got watched.

Every app had its own inbox — chat had its own saved messages, the browser had its own bookmarks, the camera roll had its own mess. Nothing talked to anything else, and the good ideas kept getting buried under the ones that didn't matter.

So the first version of rem-lays wasn't for anyone but its maker — one inbox, on every device, to capture what mattered.

That personal tool is what you're looking at now. Same idea, just ready for more than one person's mess.
