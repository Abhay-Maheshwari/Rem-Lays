1. Quick Start & Account Setup

Web Desktop Android

Rem-Lays is available anywhere you work. Set up your account once to enjoy real-time synchronization across your entire device fleet.

Authentication & Sign-in

You can sign in with one click using your Google Account or enter your email for a secure Magic Link authentication.

  • Google OAuth: Instant one-click authentication across Web, Desktop, and Android.
  • Magic Link: Passwordless sign-in delivered straight to your email inbox.
  • Session Persistence: Your authenticated session is securely cached locally so you can work offline without re-authenticating.
Pro Tip: Install the Windows or Android app to unlock native system tray quick-capture, background notifications, and home screen widgets.

2. Capturing Content

Web Desktop Android

Save text notes, rich web links, Instagram reels, photos, videos, and camera snapshots in seconds.

Content Types & Automatic Detection

The Quick Action Bar automatically parses your inputs without requiring manual type selections:

  • Quick Notes: Type markdown, code snippets, or thoughts directly into the capture bar.
  • Smart Links: Paste any URL starting with http:// or https://. Rem-Lays automatically fetches the website title, description, favicon, and rich thumbnail preview.
  • Instagram Reels & Posts: Paste Instagram links (/reel/, /p/, /tv/) to render responsive embedded players.
  • Media Uploads: Drag-and-drop images or videos directly onto your feed, or browse files with multi-select support.
  • Webcam / Direct Camera Snapping: Click the camera icon to snap a photo or record a quick video using your device camera.
  • Clipboard Paste: Hit Ctrl+V (or Cmd+V) anywhere on the app to immediately save copied images, links, or text.

Bulk Upload Queue

When dropping multiple files or pasting dozens of links at once, Rem-Lays opens a Bulk Upload Queue showing real-time file upload progress, batch tagging options, and destination board selector.

Duplicate Prevention: If you accidentally save an identical link or file, Rem-Lays notifies you immediately and highlights the existing item in your inbox.

3. Organizing & Collections

Web Desktop Android

Group related resources into neat collections, reorder items freely, and manage content in bulk.

Collections & Bundles (Groups)

Bundle multiple items together into an expandable collection card:

  • Drag-to-Group: Drag any item card onto another to create a new grouped collection.
  • Custom Title & Cover: Click on a collection to rename it, choose a custom cover image, or pick a color swatch.
  • Expandable Modal Drawer: Click any collection to view, reorder, or add items in a focused modal overlay.
  • Ungrouping: Drag an item out or choose "Ungroup" from the context menu to return items to the main feed.

Bulk Selection & Floating Toolbar

Hold Shift and click multiple cards (or tap "Select" on mobile) to activate the Bulk Actions Toolbar:

  • Batch Tagging: Add or remove tags across 50+ selected items simultaneously.
  • Move to Board: Route selected cards into any personal or shared board.
  • Pin / Star: Pin important items to stay at the top of your feed.
  • Archive / Delete: Archive completed projects or send obsolete items to the 30-day trash.

4. Tags & Instant Search

Web Desktop Android

Find any item in milliseconds with full-text search, hashtag auto-tagging, and deterministic color palettes.

Dynamic Hashtags & Color Palettes

Add tags using #tagname anywhere in your note or link descriptions. Tags automatically receive consistent, beautiful colors from a deterministic 10-palette color engine.

  • Tag Cloud Header: Click any tag in the feed header to filter your items instantly.
  • Tag Matching Modes: Toggle between AND (match all tags) or OR (match any tag) modes in the search filter bar.
  • Tag Exclusion: Hold Alt / Option and click a tag to exclude it (NOT filter).

Global Command Palette (Ctrl+K)

Press Ctrl+K (or Cmd+K on macOS) to open the Command Palette. Instantly search titles, URLs, notes, hashtags, and boards with zero keystroke delay.

5. Deadlines, Reminders & Snooze

Web Desktop Android

Keep your inbox clean by snoozing non-urgent items until you need them, and track hard deadlines effortlessly.

Snoozing Items

Click the Snooze icon on any card to hide it from your active inbox until a later time:

  • Quick Presets: Tonight (6:00 PM), Tomorrow Morning (9:00 AM), This Weekend, or Next Week.
  • Custom Date & Time: Pick an exact date and minute from the interactive picker.
  • Automatic Wakeup: When the snooze period expires, the card automatically re-appears at the top of your feed with a notification.

Task Deadlines & Visual Urgency

Attach a Deadline to any note or saved task. Rem-Lays highlights upcoming due dates with progressive urgency indicators:

  • Due in > 3 Days: Subtle badge with remaining days countdown.
  • Due Today / Tomorrow: Glowing yellow/amber card outline.
  • Overdue: Red alert banner with prominent notification banner.

6. Collaborative Boards

Web Desktop Android

Create dedicated project boards, invite teammates, and auto-route items using hashtag rules.

Board Management & Sharing

Organize your knowledge base into distinct workspaces:

  • Custom Icons & Colors: Personalize your board tabs with custom Lucide icons and accent colors.
  • Automatic Hashtag Routing: Bind tags like #design to a board so any new items with that tag are automatically categorized.
  • Granular Permissions: Share boards with Editor (can add/edit items) or Viewer (read-only access) privileges.
  • Invite Links: Generate secure 7-day invite links or invite collaborators directly via email.

7. Calendar View & Weekly Digest

Web Desktop Android

Visualize your saved links and deadlines on an interactive calendar and review past accomplishments.

Interactive Calendar & Agenda Split

Switch from Feed view to Calendar view to see your timeline at a glance:

  • Month Grid: See daily dots representing saved notes, media, and due deadlines.
  • Agenda Sidebar: Click any date to view scheduled items in an adjacent agenda list.
  • Mini-Calendar Home Widget: Collapse the calendar into a compact header widget on your home dashboard.

Weekly Digest

Every Sunday evening, Rem-Lays summarizes your top saved articles, completed deadlines, and tag analytics in a clean Weekly Digest modal.

8. Sync & Offline Mode

Web Desktop Android

Work anywhere — on an airplane, in subway tunnels, or on spotty Wi-Fi — with zero disruption.

Local-First Offline Architecture

Rem-Lays stores all items in local IndexedDB storage before syncing with the cloud:

  • Instant UI Response: Creating, editing, or deleting items updates the interface instantly with optimistic updates.
  • Offline Mutation Queue: Actions performed while disconnected are queued locally and automatically synced once a network connection is detected.
  • Active Devices Manager: In Settings, view all active desktop and mobile sessions with device names, IP locations, and remote logout options.

9. Desktop App (Tauri)

Desktop

A native, lightweight desktop app built with Tauri and Rust for Windows, macOS, and Linux.

Desktop Exclusive Features

  • Frameless Glassmorphic Window: Native OS window styling with custom window controls and acrylic blur.
  • System Tray Daemon: Minimize the app to the system tray to keep Rem-Lays running quietly in the background.
  • Launch at Startup: Toggle "Auto-start on Windows boot" in Settings to keep your vault ready at all times.
  • Native Notification Toasts with Quick Reply: When a reminder or deadline fires, an interactive toast appears with an inline quick-reply and snooze actions.

10. Android App & Home Screen Widgets

Android

Access your vault on Android with Jetpack Glance home screen widgets, system share sheet integration, and notification shade capture.

Home Screen Glance Widgets

Choose from 5 interactive home screen widgets:

  • Inbox Widget: Scrollable feed of your recent links and notes with quick checkoff.
  • Quick Capture Bar: 1-tap launcher to write a note or snap a photo from your home screen.
  • Calendar Month Widget: Full month calendar displaying deadline dots and task counts.
  • iOS-Style Split Date Widget: Minimalist current day badge with next due task.
  • Agenda Medium Widget: Upcoming 7-day schedule with one-tap snooze.

Android System Integration

  • System Share Sheet: Share links, photos, or tweets from any app directly to Rem-Lays.
  • Notification Shade Quick Capture: An ongoing pinned notification with a RemoteInput text field to save thoughts without opening the app.
  • Swipe Gestures: In the Android feed, swipe right on a card to Archive and swipe left to Snooze or Delete.

11. Sharing & 1-Click Data Export

Web Desktop Android

You own 100% of your data. Share individual links publicly or export your entire archive at any time.

Public Share Links

Click "Share" on any card or collection to generate a secret rem-lays.vercel.app/shared/:token URL. The recipient can read your notes and view media in a clean, read-only web viewer. You can revoke the share link anytime in 1 click.

1-Click Complete Data Export

Export your full vault anytime in your preferred format from Settings → Data Export:

  • Markdown (.zip): Clean markdown files with frontmatter metadata and downloaded media assets.
  • JSON (.json): Raw structured data dump ideal for developer backups or custom scripts.
  • CSV (.csv): Spreadsheet format compatible with Notion, Excel, or Google Sheets.

14. FAQ & Troubleshooting

Web Desktop Android

Answers to common setup questions and troubleshooting tips.

Why isn't a link preview showing thumbnail images?

Some websites block automatic scrapers or require login cookies (like private Twitter posts). Rem-Lays still preserves the URL, domain icon, and title.

How do I reset my local offline cache?

If you encounter sync discrepancies, navigate to Settings → Storage → Reset Cache. Your remote Supabase database will freshly populate your local storage without losing any saved data.

Can I run Rem-Lays completely self-hosted?

Yes! Rem-Lays is fully open-source under the MIT license on GitHub. You can supply your own Supabase project URL and API keys in your environment variables.
