privacy policy.

your data is yours. here is how we protect it.

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to RemLays. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use our services. By using RemLays, you agree to the terms laid out in this document.

2. Data Collection

We only collect the minimum amount of information necessary to provide and improve our service. This includes:

  • Account Information: Your email address and basic profile details when you sign up.
  • Content Data: The links, notes, screenshots, and other items you explicitly save to your inbox.
  • Usage Analytics: Anonymous, aggregated data to help us understand how the app is used so we can improve it.

3. Data Usage

Your data is used exclusively to:

  • Provide you with a seamless, synced experience across all your devices.
  • Maintain and improve the functionality of RemLays.
  • Respond to your support requests or inquiries.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

4. Data Security

All data saved in RemLays is tied to your account and visible only to you. We employ industry-standard security measures, including encryption in transit and at rest, to ensure that your information is protected against unauthorized access, alteration, or deletion.

5. Your Rights: Export and Deletion

You have full control over your data. At any time, you can:

  • Export: Download a copy of all the content you have saved in your RemLays inbox.
  • Delete: Request the complete deletion of your account and all associated data from our servers. Once deleted, this information cannot be recovered.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time as our service evolves. If we make significant changes, we will notify you through the app or via email.
