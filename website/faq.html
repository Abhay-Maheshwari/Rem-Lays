What is rem-lays?+

One inbox for everything you save — links, notes, screenshots, videos and ideas. Paste it in and tag it to stay organized.

Which platforms is it available on?+

rem-lays is available now on Windows and Android, with the same account and the same inbox synced across both. Mac and iOS are on the way — you can leave your email on the download page to get notified.

Is my data private, and can I export it?+

What you save is tied to your account and visible only to you. You can export everything at any point — nothing is locked in.

What's the difference between tags and boards?+

Tags are labels you add to keep things organized. Boards are something you build on purpose — a dedicated space for a specific project, where you decide what goes in.

Is rem-lays free?+

Yes — rem-lays is free to use right now. If that ever changes, existing users will be the first to know, well before anything does.

How do I get support?+

Email us any time — see below. We read every message and usually reply within a day or two.
