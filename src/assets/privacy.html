We use your information to provide, maintain, and improve the Rem-Lays application. We do not sell your personal data to third parties. The core purpose of collecting your email is to sync your saved links and notes securely across your own devices.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is securely stored using our backend infrastructure. We implement industry-standard security measures to ensure your inbox contents remain private and accessible only to you.

4. Third-Party Access
